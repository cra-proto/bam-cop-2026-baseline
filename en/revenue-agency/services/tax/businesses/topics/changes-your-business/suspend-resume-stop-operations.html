When your business temporarily suspends or resumes operations, it is important that you update your business account information with the CRA by calling 1-800-959-5525. We can serve you better if we have your accurate and up-to-date account information.

If your business permanently stops operating, see the topic "Closing CRA program accounts" below.

Special rules apply if you stop operating because of a receivership or bankruptcy. For more information, see the topic "Bankruptcy/Receivership" below.
