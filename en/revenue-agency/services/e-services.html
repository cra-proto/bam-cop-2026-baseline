Use the links below to get information on the digital services we offer.

Digital services for...

  • Individuals
    File or change a return, view and manage information in My Account, receive email notifications from the CRA, submit documents, calculate family benefits, make a payment, manage direct deposit, track the progress of files submitted to the CRA, and more.
  • Businesses
    File a return, make a payment, register a business, get information, make an online request, calculate payroll deductions, download or order forms and publications, submit documents, track the progress of files submitted to the CRA, and more.
  • Large corporations and multinational enterprises
    Submit an enquiry, respond to notices, submit documents, submit an audit enquiry, authorize access to online information, and more.
  • Tax preparers
    File a return or make a payment for an individual or business client, get information, subscribe to an electronic mailing list, download or order forms and publications, and more.
  • Charities and donors
    Search for a registered charity, calculate charitable donation tax credits, change a charity's address, and more.
  • Registered plan owners
    Submit an enquiry, request an amendment, inactivate or terminate a specimen, submit case listings, submit actuarial valuation reports and cost certificates, file an annual information return, request for earnings and service validation, request a waiver, administrative relief, or priority review.
